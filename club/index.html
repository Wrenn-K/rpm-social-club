01 The idea

Drive in.
Stay awhile.

RPM Social is being shaped as the digital and physical home of a modern automotive community.

The experience begins with cars, but it is designed around people. Meet over coffee, settle into the lounge, watch a race, take on a simulator challenge, browse the RPM Collection, or simply make an ordinary afternoon more interesting.

Naples gives the club its character: relaxed but considered, social without being loud, and connected to the roads, destinations, and coastal rhythm of Southwest Florida.

02 Inside RPM Social

Designed around
how people gather.

The club is planned as a connected sequence of hospitality spaces, each with its own purpose and a shared material language of warm wood, brown leather, deep green, dark metal, and soft light.

  1. 01

    Reception & Entry

    A calm arrival experience with space for a remarkable featured vehicle to set the tone.

  2. 02

    Coffee & Wine Bar

    The social center of the club, moving naturally from morning coffee to relaxed evening conversation.

  3. 03

    Main Lounge

    Comfortable seating for members, informal meetings, viewing events, and unhurried time together.

  4. 04

    Simulator Room

    A dedicated setting for casual sessions, friendly challenges, leaderboards, and race weekends.

  5. 05

    Private Event Space

    A flexible hospitality environment for companies, private groups, watch parties, and celebrations.

  6. 06

    Merchandise Area

    A considered edit of apparel, drinkware, accessories, and limited RPM Social releases.
