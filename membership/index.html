02 The value of belonging

More access.
More connection.

Membership is being designed to make RPM Social feel like part of your routine—not merely a venue you visit for occasional events.

01

Make the lounge yours

Use the club as a place to meet, work informally, watch, unwind, and connect during eligible access hours.

02

Be closer to the calendar

Member access and preferred opportunities are planned across selected events, socials, and experiences.

03

Keep the competition friendly

Simulator sessions, challenges, and leaderboards are planned as an ongoing part of member life.

04

Go beyond Naples

Higher membership concepts may include priority access to curated drives and future travel opportunities.
