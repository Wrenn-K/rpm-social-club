01 The club

A place built for
more than the drive.

RPM Social brings automotive culture and thoughtful hospitality together under one roof.

The vision is a place where enthusiasts can arrive in something they love, settle in over coffee, watch a major event, challenge a friend on the simulators, or simply spend an unhurried afternoon in good company.

Cars are the common language, not the admission test. RPM is being shaped to feel just as welcoming to the curious passenger, the coffee regular, and the lifelong enthusiast.

02 Inside RPM Social

Designed around
how people gather.

The club is planned as a sequence of warm, connected spaces—each distinct in purpose, all part of one relaxed experience.

  1. 01

    Reception & Entry

    A calm arrival, a clear welcome, and room for a remarkable car to set the tone.

  2. 02

    Coffee & Wine Bar

    The social center of the club, designed for morning coffee and easy evening conversation.

  3. 03

    Main Lounge

    Comfortable seating for members, informal meetings, viewing events, and time well spent.

  4. 04

    Simulator Room

    A dedicated setting for casual sessions, friendly challenges, and race-weekend energy.

  5. 05

    Private Events

    A flexible hospitality environment for companies, private groups, watch parties, and celebrations.

  6. 06

    RPM Collection

    A considered retail edit of apparel, drinkware, accessories, and limited seasonal drops.
